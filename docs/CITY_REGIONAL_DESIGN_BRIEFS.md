# City design briefs for the 64 regional descriptions

Prepared 1 November 2026 as the qualitative regional authoring matrix for the
[city implementation plan](CITY_BUILDINGS_IMPLEMENTATION_PLAN.md).

These proposals interpret the written descriptions and geographic scope in
[the 64-region catalogue](REGIONAL_ARCHITECTURAL_THEMES.md) and its source
[environments.json](../server/buildgen/catalog/environments.json). They do not
inherit the existing defined styles, compiled profiles, dimensions, colours,
material weights, grammar identifiers or expression probabilities. All entries
currently have status `text_based_proposal`.

Geography below is abbreviated; retain the full source coverage and its local
distinctions during authoring. The city and public-space columns are our proposed
translations, not assertions about measured local construction or landscaping.
Where source text primarily describes houses, use it selectively at a compatible
street frontage or entrance. A neutral contemporary office/hotel family remains
available in every region; no region establishes the presence of a tower or hotel.

Apply source evidence and physical fit first. Courts, galleries, terraces and
covered walks need supported geometry and accepted access. Planting needs actual
residual space, local climate context and supported assets; none of the entries
specifies species, a road marking scheme, accessibility dimensions or structural
standards. A rain/shade/shelter direction is a design question to resolve for the
local site, not an instruction to alter all buildings in the region.

## Implementation handoff and review

For each ID, C0/C1 must retain its source text and explicit city interpretation,
record supported locality/family/zone combinations, and identify any neutral or
inapplicable result. Numerical palettes, facade dimensions and selection weights
are future city-specific authoring work. Review new parameters against the brief
and actual city geometry; do not fill gaps from the defined residential styles.

All 64 IDs require a coverage result. Visual comparisons should include the same
office/hotel family in several compatible regions, multiple expressions within a
region, and public-base versus upper-tower treatment. Compare common materials to
assess composition, then fixed geometry to assess new material/paving/planting
interpretations. Preserve unknown-locality and source-override controls.

This matrix does not change current regional routing, active ordinary-building
styles or runtime assets. The main plan defines the eventual integration,
dependency, regression, performance and game-review gates.
